Generative AI in software development:
real gain or illusion?

Coding assistants built on generative AI are now part of many developers' daily work. This watch follows what they really bring, what they may degrade (quality, security) and the framework in which to use them. It is directly tied to my future job.

Three themes

Productivity, quality, framework

Theme 01

Productivity gains: perceived or measured?

The DORA 2025 report (nearly 5,000 professionals) says more than 80% believe AI has increased their productivity. Yet the METR study on real tasks by experienced developers measured the opposite: 19% more time, while they expected to save 24%. The study dates from 2025 and covers the tools of the time: the gain depends on context, experience and tools.

Theme 02

Quality and security of generated code

According to Veracode's summer 2026 leaderboard (11 models; 100+ tracked since the programme began), models pass 56% of security tests on average, almost unchanged from a year ago: Python reaches 63%, Java only 30%. These tests cover raw models, with no human review or guardrails. DORA describes AI as an amplifier: it strengthens an organisation's strengths but also its weaknesses, and delivery instability keeps rising.

Theme 03

Responsible use and legal framework

In the 2026 Stack Overflow survey, 78.7% say source attribution matters and only 24% say their employer has approved tools or an official policy. On the legal side, the CNIL published its recommendations in July 2025 for developing GDPR-compliant AI systems, noting that the GDPR applies in many cases to models trained on personal data. A habit worth keeping: never paste API keys or personal data into an assistant.

My takeaways

AI speeds up writing, not responsibility.

  • Review, test, understand. Generated code is checked before it is shipped, especially for security.
  • Measure rather than believe. Perceived productivity is no substitute for measurement.
  • Keep the fundamentals. For a student, the assistant must not replace learning: without the fundamentals, it is impossible to judge what it suggests.
