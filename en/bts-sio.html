The BTS SIO, SLAM option.

IT Services for Organisations: a two-year diploma leading to two careers, application developer or infrastructure administrator. I chose development. Here is the diploma, its two options and why SLAM.

One diploma, two specialities.

The BTS SIO trains for digital jobs in organisations. The two options share a common core, then specialise: three skill blocks, of which only one really changes with the option.

Block 1

Support and delivery of IT services

Managing the IT assets, handling incidents and requests, working on projects, making a service available to users.

Block 2 · depends on the option

Application design or network administration

This is where the two options split: developing applications (SLAM) or administering systems and networks (SISR).

Block 3

Cybersecurity of IT services

Protecting personal data and securing equipment and usage against cyberattacks. The content adapts to the option: applications on one side, infrastructure on the other.

General subjects

General culture, English, mathematics, economics and law

They complete the technical training and prepare for working in a team, with customers and users.

My option

SLAM — Software solutions and business applications.

The developers' option. You learn to analyse a need, design an application, program it, test it, deploy it and maintain it, with data security in mind.

What you learn

Object-oriented programming, application architecture, databases, APIs, testing, version control with Git, modelling and project methods.

PHPJavaScriptSQLGitObject-orientedAPI

What I did with it

My first-year internship: a complete alumni platform with Next.js, React, MySQL and deployment on Vercel, from analysing the need to going live.

Next.jsReactMySQLVercel

Target jobs

Application developer, web or full-stack developer, analyst-programmer, integrator, application support officer.

Cybersecurity on the code side

Roles and permissions, authentication, secrets kept out of the code, personal data protection: security is designed in from the start.

OAuth 2.0GDPRRoles

SISR — Infrastructure, systems and networks solutions.

The other option: the administrators' one. You install, configure, monitor and protect the systems and networks that keep an organisation's services running.

What you learn

Windows and Linux systems, networks and protocols, virtualisation, directories, backups, monitoring, firewalls.

Windows ServerLinuxNetworksVirtualisationMonitoring

Target jobs

Systems and networks technician or administrator, infrastructure technician, support and operations, a first step toward cybersecurity.

Cybersecurity on the infrastructure side

Segmenting a network, filtering traffic, hardening servers, managing access and watching logs.

FirewallVLANAccess

Why SLAM?

I like building something concrete that serves people. My internship confirmed it: seeing the alumni platform live, used by real users, is what motivates me most. My IT degree year gave me the programming basics, and the BTS gives me practice and method.

After the BTS.

Professional bachelor's

A specialisation in development or cybersecurity, often as a work-study programme.

Bachelor's or engineering school

Further study is possible, depending on the application, to reach a three- to five-year level.

Entering the job market

Junior developer, integrator, application support. This is my goal: full-stack web developer.
