The BTS SIO, SLAM option.

IT Services for Organisations: a two-year diploma leading to two careers, application developer or infrastructure administrator. I chose development. Here is the diploma, its two options and why SLAM.

Two years, from the core to the speciality.

1st year

The basics and a first internship

Programming (Java, Python, C), databases, server and network basics (Windows, Linux, Cisco) and cybersecurity. First 5-week internship: the ENC Bessières alumni platform.

2nd year

Specialisation and the second internship

Option projects, cybersecurity, exam preparation. I am looking for my internship from 9 November to 18 December 2026.

In total, at least 10 weeks of internship over the two years.

A common core, then two paths.

Common core

Support and delivery of IT services (block 1), cybersecurity of IT services (block 3), English, general culture, mathematics, economics and law.

Why SLAM?

I chose development for three reasons.

  • Building something concrete.What motivates me is creating a tool people use. My internship confirmed it: the alumni platform is live.
  • A natural next step.My IT degree year gave me a taste for programming. SLAM brings the practice and method of a real project.
  • Security in the code.Cybersecurity interests me, and in SLAM it is built into the application: roles, authentication, personal data.

My goal: to become a full-stack web developer.
